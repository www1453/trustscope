## 0. The one-paragraph answer

The frames are **demosaiced RGB888, not Bayer** (four-parity means differ by at most 0.40 grey
on any of the 75 frames; a mosaic cannot look like that), so no demosaic handling is needed and
no ratio here is a ratio of an interpolated plane. The pre-registered statistic -- per pixel
`|B_live/G_live - B_ref/G_ref|`, per cell its plain mean, per board the maximum over judged
cells -- reads **0.214 median on the 40 good boards and 0.640 (g20) at the good maximum**, and
**0.268 median / 0.590 (d30) at the scratch maximum**; at the pre-registered threshold the
detection is **0 of 12**, with **AUC 0.623** (95% CI 0.398-0.829 -- the highest point estimate
any statistic has reached on this class, though the interval still includes 0.5). So the **verdict is negative: the criterion was 0 of 40 good and at
least 8 of 12 scratch, and it is 0 and 0**. The axis's physics is real and is measured here: on
d30's five known line cells the ratio rises **4%-67%**, the scratch raises the blue level by
**+32% to +109%** against **+8% to +34%** in green, so the 2.09x blue lead reported earlier is
confirmed and even a little larger. What kills it is that the response of this statistic is
`q_ref * (relative B/G change)`: the scratch's relative change (0.67) is **larger** than the
worst good board's (0.55), but the scratch sits on a patch whose reference B/G is 0.53 while
the good board's difference sits on a patch whose reference B/G is 1.07, so the good board
**out-scores** the scratch (0.592 against 0.357). The scratch's best cell is only at the
**99.65th percentile** of 18,609 good judged cells -- below the 99.9th -- so no cell-level
threshold can hold 0/40 and 8/12 at once: reaching 8/12 would take a threshold of 0.22495, at
which **18 of 40 good boards** are above it. The intensity control (`G/G_ref`) finds **1/12**
-- *more* than the primary, which is the opposite of the hypothesis, and is the strongest single
statement that the blue channel is a liability in the ratio form rather than an asset. The
scatter control fails its pre-registered usability rule (flat good cells: median 0.0414, 99th
percentile 0.202, against the dimmest d30 line cell 0.126). The line is locatable independently
on **1 of 12** boards and nominated by the record on 4 of 12; the pre-registered 12-board
number is the verdict. **No firmware change is proposed. This closes the per-pixel spectral
ratio family, as the four earlier families were closed.**

## 1. Why this axis was worth the tenth attempt, and what was frozen

Nine rounds measured **|live - reference| in grey or in a single channel** -- one axis, in
amplitude form. On it the scratch's signature (58 grey at the line, only 1.3-1.6x the cell
mean) is smaller than the unit-to-unit difference between two good boards at specular pads
(168-200 grey), and nothing separated: 0/12 for the cell/tap shape statistics, 0/12 for the
full-cell maximum, 0/12 for R and G and B amplitude alike, 2/12 for the record's DAREA
(`SHAPE_20261029.md`, `PIXELMAX_20261029.md`, `connarea_20261030/numbers.txt`).

This round tests a **ratio**, not an amplitude. A specular intensity swing multiplies the pixel;
a quotient of two channels at the same pixel is unchanged by a common multiplier. If the good
boards differ mostly by such a swing, the ratio should be quiet on them, while the scratch --
measured to have a real spectral signature, B carrying 2.09x the contrast of G at d30's line --
should survive. **That is a different axis, and it was frozen as such.**

Frozen in the pre-registration, before any number: the populations (the same 40 good and the
same 12 scratch boards as the three prior rounds, so the numbers are comparable), the geometry
(the registration **imported unchanged** from `dl/pixelmax_20261029/pixelmax.py`), the
photometric model (**none** -- a correction would remove the very effect under test), the
survival floor (`min(G_live, G_ref) >= 20`), the judged-cell conditions, the cell value (plain
mean of the 64 pixel ratios), the primary aggregator (`RMAX`, max over cells), the criterion
(0/40 and 8/12), one secondary aggregator (`RT5`, mean of the 5 largest cells -- justified by
the measured 5-cell extent of d30's line, not chosen after the fact), the two controls with
their readings written down in advance, the bayer checks, and the honest-bound reporting.

## 2. Instruments, and the one that is reused rather than rewritten

**The registration is that round's code.** `register`, `T_of`, `bilin`, `box` and `Pyramid` are
imported from `dl/pixelmax_20261029/pixelmax.py`; nothing about the transform, the initialiser
or the two Nelder-Mead refinements was re-implemented or re-tuned for this statistic.

| check | what | result |
|---|---|---|
| **I2** | that round's two synthetic known-answer transforms | **2/2 exact** (0.000 px, 0.000 deg, 0.000000 scale) |
| **I1** | recover the same transform on all 52 real boards | **max \|delta\| = 0.000e+00** over 52 boards |

**A note on I1's wording, reported rather than hidden.** The pre-registration wrote the
reference as `dl/pixelmax_20261029/table.csv` at 1e-6. That file is a *rendering* printed at
3/3/4/6 decimals, so it cannot be compared at 1e-6 -- the first run of the check stopped at
4.9e-04, which is exactly that file's rounding half-width. The check was therefore applied to
the full-precision `results.json` that produced the table, where it gives **exactly zero** on
all 52 boards. The pre-registration's wording was imprecise; the check it asked for passes
exactly, against the file that holds the values.

No board was excluded: all 52 register, 380-647 judged cells per board.

## 3. Bayer or demosaiced RGB? (the task's item 4, answered before any positive)

**B1, the four-parity test.** For a Bayer mosaic three quarters of each channel's samples are
interpolated from another channel's sites, so the four `(x mod 2, y mod 2)` classes of a channel
differ by tens of grey. For a demosaiced frame they differ only by noise.

| channel | reference parity spread | worst spread over the 75 live frames |
|---|---|---|
| R | 0.358 grey | 0.398 |
| G | 0.288 grey | 0.333 |
| B | 0.210 grey | 0.258 |

**The frames are demosaiced RGB888, not Bayer.** All 225 channel-frame spreads are below 0.4
grey against a 2.0 grey decision threshold. This is the same conclusion the previous round
reached on the reference alone (<= 0.36 grey); it is re-established here on every frame this
study uses, because a ratio of a Bayer-interpolated plane could synthesise structure that is
not in the scene, and that hazard had to be excluded before a result could be read at all.

**B2, the demosaic sensitivity, since the question was asked.** The stored planes were
re-mosaiced to RGGB and rebuilt by the standard bilinear (3x3 normalised box) reconstruction,
and the **good-side null was recomputed at the same transforms and the same judged cells**, so
only the demosaic changed:

| planes | good-side `RMAX` |
|---|---|
| as stored (demosaiced RGB) | **0.64046** |
| re-mosaiced + bilinearly demosaiced | **0.63831** (x0.997) |

The reconstruction moves the null by 0.3%. So: (i) the frames are not bayer, (ii) even if they
were, this statistic would not be picking up interpolation structure at cell scale, and (iii) no
positive here could have been a demosaic artefact -- and there is no positive.

## 4. Result: the primary and the one secondary

Threshold: `tau = max over the 40 good boards`, strictly greater.

| statistic | good min / median / max | scratch min / median / max | `tau` | good above | scratch above | AUC [95% CI] |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`RMAX`** (primary) | 0.0749 / 0.2137 / **0.6405** | 0.1122 / 0.2682 / **0.5903** | 0.64046 | 0/40 | **0/12** | **0.623** [0.398, 0.829] |
| **`RT5`** (secondary) | 0.0687 / 0.1946 / 0.6049 | 0.1024 / 0.2315 / 0.5692 | 0.60486 | 0/40 | **0/12** | 0.590 [0.379, 0.796] |
| `RMAX` with the board's median offset removed (declared normalisation sensitivity, section 7 of the pre-registration) | 0.0749 / 0.2207 / 0.6089 | 0.1178 / 0.2871 / 0.5904 | 0.60893 | 0/40 | **0/12** | 0.619 [0.402, 0.825] |

**Verdict: does not separate.** 0 of 12 at the only 0-good point, against a bar of 8 of 12. The
gap is not marginal: the **8th-largest scratch value is 0.2265**, which is **0.414 below** the
threshold. The scratch maximum (0.5903, d30) is below the good maximum (0.6405, g20), and d30's
own largest cell is the only scratch value above 0.40.

This is, however, the **highest point estimate of any statistic this project has tried on this
class** (the previous best were the record's DAREA at 0.600 and the four-tap shape statistic at
0.542), so the axis is carrying information -- but the interval still **includes 0.5**
(0.398-0.829), so at 95% confidence even the above-chance claim is not established, and the
criterion does not depend on it. The scratch distribution *is* shifted: median 0.268 against
0.214. The failure is entirely in the **good tail**.

**Leave-one-out** (threshold = the maximum of the other 39 good boards): the median scratch
detection is **0**, the maximum over the 40 held-out splits is **1**, and 1 of 40 held-out good
boards is above its own threshold. So the 0/12 is not an artefact of choosing the maximum of the
40 as the threshold.

**The ROC path says what the criterion costs.** Every threshold that reaches 8/12 also admits
good boards:

| threshold | good above /40 | scratch above /12 |
|---|---|---|
| > 0.64046 (`tau`) | 0 | **0** |
| > 0.59029 | 1 | 0 |
| > 0.33365 | 1 | 1 |
| > 0.31714 | 2 | 3 |
| > 0.26589 | 9 | 6 |
| > 0.22495 | **18** | **8** |
| > 0.21050 | 22 | 9 |
| > 0.19226 | 29 | 9 |
| > 0.14192 | 36 | 10 |
| > 0.07486 | 39 | 12 |

**To reach 8 of 12 the threshold must fall to 0.22495, where 18 of 40 good boards -- 45% -- are
above it.** The class is not linearly separable on this statistic; the 0-good requirement is
what fails, and it fails by a factor of two in board count.

## 5. The intensity control: the result is the opposite of the hypothesis

The identical pipeline with `G_live/G_ref` in place of `B/G` (u = |G_live/G_ref - 1|), same
transforms, same judged cells, same floor, same aggregation:

| statistic | good max | scratch max | `tau` | good above | scratch above | AUC [95% CI] |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| primary `B/G` | 0.6405 | 0.5903 (d30) | 0.64046 | 0/40 | **0/12** | 0.623 [0.398, 0.829] |
| **intensity control `G/G`** | **0.4621** | **0.6540 (d30)** | 0.46211 | 0/40 | **1/12** | 0.504 [0.292, 0.706] |

**The single-channel ratio separates one board and the two-channel ratio separates none.** That
is not a marginal difference in the predicted direction; it is the reverse of it.

Why, with numbers. At d30's five line cells the two statistics read:

| cell | `B/G` | `G/G` | `G/G` / `B/G` |
|---|---|---|---|
| (45,73) | 0.1261 | 0.0804 | 0.64 |
| (46,73) | 0.3237 | 0.2436 | 0.75 |
| (46,74) | 0.3522 | 0.2520 | 0.72 |
| (46,75) | 0.2358 | 0.3429 | **1.45** |
| (46,76) | 0.1511 | 0.2970 | **1.97** |

On three of the five line cells `B/G` does read 25%-36% larger than `G/G`, in the direction the
2.09x lead predicts; on the two dimmest it is the other way by a factor of 2. But the decisive
comparison is not at the line, it is on the good side: **the B/G primary raises the good maximum
by 39% over the control (0.6405 against 0.4621) while it lowers the scratch maximum by 10%
(0.5903 against 0.6540).** Adding blue adds more noise to the good side than signal to the
scratch side. In the ratio form the blue channel is a liability, which is the opposite of what
the amplitude measurement suggested -- because the amplitude measurement never had to confront
the good boards' own blue differences (section 7).

The three-way reading pre-registered in section 5 of the pre-registration covered "both pass",
"only B/G passes", "neither passes". The measured case is a fourth one -- **only the control
passes, and only by one board** -- and it is reported as such rather than mapped onto one of the
three.

## 6. The scatter control: the pre-registered rule says the axis is unusable

Flat cells are the judged cells whose **reference** texture is at or below the board's median
(a median split, so no constant was chosen by looking at the data). Pooled over the 40 good
boards, n = 9,317 on d30's line-free neighbourhoods:

| flat good cells | value |
|---|---|
| median | **0.04141** |
| 90th percentile | 0.08582 |
| **99th percentile** | **0.20221** |
| maximum | 0.50360 |

d30's line cells run **0.12614 to 0.35224**.

**The pre-registered usability rule** was: usable iff the 99th percentile of the flat good cells
is below one half of the smallest d30 line cell. Half of 0.12614 is **0.06307**; the measured
99th percentile is **0.20221**.

**The rule fails: the axis is UNUSABLE by its own pre-registered test**, and that finding stands
independently of the primary. The honest reading of both numbers together: the ratio's *typical*
flat-cell noise (0.0414) is 3x to 8.5x below the line's cells, so the line is genuinely
measurable; but its *tail* is not -- **1% of flat good cells already exceed d30's dimmest line
cell**, so a threshold that must hold 0 of 40 boards cannot be set on flat-cell noise. A
threshold placed above the flat-cell 99th percentile would sit at 0.202 and still be reached by
1% of flat cells alone; the board-level maximum over ~450 cells then routinely reaches 0.4-0.6
on boards with no defect, which is exactly the good maximum observed (0.6405).

**The pre-registered conclusion is therefore stronger than "0/12": even a perfect line locator
could not turn this statistic into a 0-good rule at this aggregation.**

## 7. Mechanism: the lead is real, and it is not where the maximum is

### 7.1 The scratch's spectral signature is confirmed, in relative terms

At d30's five known line cells, cell means of the live and reference channels:

| cell | live G | live B | ref G | ref B | `dG_rel` | `dB_rel` | `q_live` = B/G | `q_ref` = B/G |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (45,73) | 149.4 | 102.6 | 138.9 | 77.5 | +0.076 | **+0.324** | 0.687 | 0.558 |
| (46,73) | 164.8 | 143.6 | 133.0 | 72.7 | +0.239 | **+0.977** | 0.872 | 0.546 |
| (46,74) | 158.5 | 140.8 | 126.9 | 67.5 | +0.249 | **+1.087** | 0.888 | 0.531 |
| (46,75) | 163.6 | 147.2 | 121.8 | 80.5 | +0.343 | **+0.828** | 0.900 | 0.661 |
| (46,76) | 153.9 | 133.1 | 119.9 | 99.6 | +0.284 | **+0.336** | 0.865 | 0.831 |

The scratch raises blue by **32% to 109%** and green by only **8% to 34%**: the relative blue
change is **2.2x to 4.9x** the green change. The 2.09x figure measured in the previous round is
confirmed, and in relative terms it is larger still. **The scratch's optical signature is not in
doubt and was never the weak link.**

### 7.2 The maximum is not the line -- again, one level up

d30's board value `RMAX = 0.5903` is at cell **(49,55)**, which is **21 cells away** from the
nearest line cell. Its five line cells peak at **0.3522**. So on d30 the statistic that fires
hardest is not the defect -- the same failure the full-cell maximum showed at (67,66), one
statistic later.

### 7.3 Why: the statistic's response is `q_ref` x the relative change

For any pixel, `|q_live - q_ref| = q_ref * |q_live/q_ref - 1|`. The second factor is the
sensible, scale-free quantity; the **first is the local reference B/G**, and the reference is
chromatically heterogeneous (green solder mask, blue-ish pads and metal). The two competing
cells, side by side:

| | relative B/G change | reference `q_ref` | **statistic** |
|---|---|---|---|
| d30's line, cell (46,74) | **0.672** (the larger of the two) | 0.531 (green-dominant patch) | **0.357** |
| g20's worst cell (43,67) | 0.553 | **1.070** (blue-dominant patch) | **0.592** |

**The scratch's relative change is larger than the good board's, and it scores 40% lower**,
because it is measured on a patch where blue is already weak. g20's cell is a genuine
unit-to-unit difference -- the reference board has B 117.4 and G 109.7 there (blue-dominant),
good board g20 has B 59.1 and G 123.6 (green-dominant), with no clipping (clipped-pixel fraction
0.00) and a neighbouring cell at 0.624, so it is a patch, not a hot pixel.

This is the exact sense in which the axis is *not* spectrally invariant: **the ratio cancels a
common multiplier, and the thing that varies between two units of this product is not common --
it is a chromatic difference at metallic and specular features.** The measurement is consistent
with that and nothing else: the good side's tail is where the failures are, in 0.1% of cells
(q99.9 = 0.454) rather than across the board.

### 7.4 How far the line gets, in the good population

Pooled over all 40 good boards, **18,609 judged cells**: median 0.0497, 90th 0.1169, 99th
0.2428, 99.9th 0.4544, maximum 0.6405. d30's line cells sit at the **91.9, 99.6, 99.7, 98.9 and
95.2** percentiles. The best of them -- cell (46,74) at **0.3522** -- has only **65 of the 18,609
good cells (0.35%)** above it, and is still **below the 99.9th percentile**, i.e. below about 19
good cells.

**That is the decisive number of this round.** A detector that reads one cell cannot hold
0/40 and 8/12 here: 0.1% of good cells are already above the scratch's best cell, and a board
contributes ~450 cells to the maximum.

## 11. Sensitivity: is the negative one board's artefact?

g20 is the good board that sets `tau`, and g20 is also the board the previous round flagged as
having a multi-modal registration cost surface. Removing it:

| | `tau` | scratch detected |
|---|---|---|
| all 40 good | 0.64046 | 0/12 |
| 39 good (g20 removed) | 0.33365 | **1/12** (d30 0.5903) |

**Even excluding the worst good board, the detection is 1 of 12, not 8.** The negative does not

## 12. What a positive would have cost -- and why nothing is proposed

Because the verdict is negative, **no firmware change is proposed**. The cost is recorded only
so the engineering ledger is complete, in the same form the previous round used.

A per-pixel ratio needs, per judged cell, the blue byte beside the green one on **both** sides.
Today the PS fine pass (`ps_ui/main.c:10069-10154`) reads 36 live green bytes through
`cell_mean8_ex()` and calls `ref_cell8()`, which does 4 taps x 9 `ref_at()` calls; `ref_at()`
(`main.c:8977-9002`) reads **only the green byte** (`(uint32_t)iy*CAM_STRIDE + ix*3 + 1`) and
does four weighted reads per sample. So a judged cell today costs 36 live byte reads plus 144
reference byte reads -- 180. Adding blue at the **same 36 samples** doubles that to **360**
(2.0x). Adding blue at the **64 pixels this measurement actually uses** costs 64 G + 64 B live
reads and 4x that on the reference side, i.e. **640 reads, 3.6x**. Either way it needs a second
bilinear sampler for blue beside `ref_at()`, in the pass that already costs 91.1 ms per
comparison (`MB_DIFF_MS`, Stage 8). It needs no new PL block and no new DDR buffer -- which is
why it *would* have been cheap -- but it is not cheap enough to implement for a statistic that
separates nothing.

**A bench confirmation is not required, because there is nothing to confirm.** What a future
round would need if it wanted to retest this axis: a new reference take (which shifts the whole
DAREA column), >= 40 good and the same 12 scratch boards, the same five evidence files, and this
script unchanged.

## 13. What this closes, and what it does not claim

**Closes:** the per-pixel spectral-ratio family, in the form "difference of B/G", at this
geometry, on this population, against this reference. It fails the pre-registered criterion at
0/12, on both aggregators, on both photometric models, and with the one board that carries the
whole signal removed it still reaches only 1/12. Together with the four earlier families this
means: **on this bench, at this registration quality, the scratch class is not separable from
40 good boards of the same product by any per-pixel statistic in grey, in a single channel, or
in a channel ratio.** That is now a measured statement about four axes, not one.

**Does not claim:**
- that no statistic can ever separate this class -- only that these four families, computed this
  way on this population, do not;
- that the scratch is invisible -- it is visible, and this study measures its spectral signature
  more sharply than the previous round did (+32% to +109% in blue at the line, 92nd-99.7th
  percentile of good cells);
- that the good boards' chromatic differences are "defects" -- they are unit-to-unit surface
  differences on a product whose pads and mask are not spectrally uniform;
- that the registration is exact -- it is exact for the *green* channel, which is what it
  minimises; it was never fitted on blue, and the blue residuals are not part of I1;
- that a `q_ref`-normalised variant would fail -- **it is not tested here**. Section 7.3 shows
  the statistic is sensitive to the local reference B/G level, and the two competing cells
  suggest the scale-free form would order them the other way. That is a hypothesis for a future
  round to pre-register and test on all 52 boards; **it is not a result of this one and it is not
  substituted for the verdict**;

## 14. Reproduction and run time

```
python dl\ratio_20261030\ratio.py --null          # the 40-good side, hashed
python dl\ratio_20261030\ratio.py --defect        # refuses unless the hash matches
python dl\ratio_20261030\mechanism.py             # post-hoc mechanism + numbers.txt
python dl\ratio_20261030\figures.py               # the three figures
```

or the whole primary path as one command, `python dl\ratio_20261030\ratio.py --all`.

**Measured run times on this machine:** `--null` 260.0 s, `--defect` 260.3 s, `--all` **288.0 s**
(the two-stage version repeats the gates and the 75 registrations; `--all` does them once),
`mechanism.py` **2.0 s** (it reuses the frozen transforms and re-registers nothing),
`figures.py` **1.9 s**. The log of the timed `--all` run is `run_all_timed.txt`. The statistics
are deterministic: a `--all` re-run reproduced every per-board value bit for bit (checked field
by field against the frozen `results.json` and `null_good.json`; the only difference is each
board's wall-time field, and therefore the file hash -- the quoted `null_good.json` hash
`d84fbef9...` is the hash of the run whose artifacts are shipped here).

The bootstrap seed is 20261030 (2,000 resamples, both classes, percentile 2.5/97.5). No
randomness enters the statistic.

## 16. 中文摘要

**结论：不分离。** 帧是**去马赛克后的 RGB888，不是 Bayer**（75 帧中任一通道的四种奇偶位均值
最大只差 0.40 灰；Bayer 不可能如此），所以不需要任何去马赛克处理，"插值平面之比会造假结构"
这条路已经排除。预注册统计量——逐像素 `|B/G_live − B/G_ref|`、逐格 64 像素的普通均值、板级取
最大 `RMAX`——良品 40 块中位 0.214、最大 **0.640（g20）**；划伤 12 块中位 0.268、最大
**0.590（d30）**；在"零良品超过"的阈值上检出 **0/12**，**AUC 0.623**（95% CI 0.398–0.829，
是本项目在这个类别上测到过的**最高点估计**，但区间仍包含 0.5）。判据是"良品 0/40 且划伤 ≥8/12"，
实测 0 和 0，**不分离**。次要聚合
`RT5`（最大 5 格均值）同样 0/12；去掉板级中位偏移的归一化版本 0/12。留一法：检出中位 0、
最大 1/12，留出良品 1/40。

**这条轴的物理是真的。** 在 d30 已知的 5 个划伤格上，比值上升 4%–67%；蓝通道相对变化
**+32%~+109%**，绿只有 **+8%~+34%**（相对蓝/绿 = **2.2~4.9 倍**），上一轮测到的 2.09 倍由此
得到确认，而且更大。失败的是**良品侧**：比值噪声的中位数只有 0.0414，但尾部 q99 = 0.202、
q99.9 = 0.454。**d30 最好的划伤格（0.352）只落在 18609 个良品格中的第 99.65 百分位，
低于第 99.9 百分位**——任何"读单格"的判据都无法同时做到 0/40 与 8/12：要拿到 8/12，阈值必须
降到 0.22495，那时 **40 块良品里有 18 块（45%）超过它**。

**为什么失败（机制，带数字）：** 统计量的响应等于 `q_ref × 相对变化`。划痕的相对变化
（0.672）其实**大于**最差良品板 g20 的（0.553），但划痕落在参考 B/G 只有 0.531 的绿主导区，
g20 的差异落在参考 B/G 高达 1.070 的蓝主导区，于是良品算出来 0.592、划痕只有 0.357。
**比值能消掉"共模乘性"变化，而两块板之间真正变化的是金属/镜面处的"色度"差异，不是共模的。**
而且 d30 板级最大值在格 (49,55)，距划痕 21 格——最大值再次不是缺陷本身。

**两个对照**：亮度对照 `G/G` 良品最大 0.462、划伤最大 0.654（d30），**1/12**——单通道比值的
检出反而**多于**双通道主统计量；加入蓝通道让良品侧涨了 39%、划伤侧只涨了 10%（不升反降）。
散布对照：按预注册规则，平坦良品格 q99 = 0.202 **不小于**最暗划伤格的一半（0.063），
**判定为不可用**。

**划痕可定位的板数**：独立定位 **1/12**（仅 d30）；按记录中 ≥5 格连通分量提名 **4/12**
（d23、d29、d30、d32）；提名的 4 块超过阈值 **0/4**，其余 8 块 **0/8**。去掉 g20 后阈值降到
0.33365，检出仍只有 **1/12**——负结果不依赖任何一块板。

**代价与结论**：在阈值 0.64046 上污染板 21 块中 2 块超过、边角板 0/2 超过。
**不建议任何固件改动**（若当初通过，代价是同样 36 个采样点上字节流量翻倍 2.0×，按本次实测的
64 像素格算则 3.6×：蓝通道需要第二个双线性采样器，现有 `ref_at` 只读绿字节 `+1`）。**本族到此关闭**：灰度量级、
单通道、形状、全格最大、以及通道比值，五族都测过，都是 0/12。

## 18. Bottom line for the owner

- The frames are **demosaiced RGB**, verified on all 75; no demosaic sensitivity exists, and a
  re-mosaic/re-demosaic recompute moves the null by 0.3%.
- The **B/G ratio does not separate the scratch class**: 0/12 at the 0-good point, AUC 0.623.
  Reaching 8/12 would admit 18 of 40 good boards.
- The scratch's spectral signature is **real and is now measured in relative terms**: blue rises
  by up to +109% at its line while green rises by +34%. It was never the missing piece.
- **The missing piece is that two units of the same product differ chromatically at pads and
  metal by more than the scratch differs from a clean surface** -- and a channel ratio, unlike a
  difference, reports that chromatic difference faithfully instead of cancelling it.
- The single-channel control beats the two-channel statistic (1/12 against 0/12). **Blue is a
  liability in the ratio form.**
- **Do not implement this.** No firmware change, no bench run, no production threshold. The
  per-pixel spectral ratio family is closed.
