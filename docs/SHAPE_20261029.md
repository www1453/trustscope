# Shape/structure statistics on the stored frames: the scratch class is not separable by shape at the cell geometry, and the per-pixel variant is not in the record

2026-10-29, offline analysis session. **The station was OFF and was not touched**: no board, no
xsdb, no `ps_ui/` change, no build, no flash. The frames under
`dl/sweep_frames/20261029_181623_sweep/` and the CSVs were read only. Work directory
`dl/shape_scan/`.

Frozen pre-registration: [`PREREG_20261029T2120.md`](shape_scan/PREREG_20261029T2120.md), sha256
`941866e808254d0c88a279fc0a83981e3e20d375f6362eb4452a66fac380ea29`, written before any
per-board statistic in this study. Amendment A1
([`PREREG_AMENDMENT_A1.md`](shape_scan/PREREG_AMENDMENT_A1.md), sha256
`c92c917bdff004216366fca69b9f13a50c0aa25920d5521c8f13565d36430023`) records that the
frame set covers **40** good boards, not 41 (`g38` has no evidence directory), and changes
only the denominator. The good-side null was computed and hashed before the scratch side:
`null_good.json` sha256 `f02fe2dd1ae010a2005bdd1c7e8819418a192f9a7782f31e8726a362936aacb6`;
`shape_scan.py --defect` refuses to run unless that hash matches.

---

## 0. The one-paragraph answer

**No.** The pre-registered shape statistics do not separate the 12 scratch boards from the 40
good frames, and they fail in the same direction: the good boards carry **more** texture and
**more** deep cells than the scratches. The four-tap spread reads 112–165 grey on the good
boards and 97–149 on the scratches (AUC 0.39); the largest-component elongation reads 0–4.39
on the good boards and 0–2.53 on the scratches (AUC 0.54); the live-frame top-hat line length
reads 0–36.5 px on the good boards and 0–29.6 px on the scratches (AUC 0.39). At the
pre-registered "zero good boards above the threshold" point the detection is **0/12** for all
three; the best any secondary aggregator achieves is **2/12**, and that point is the frame
DAREA rule, not a shape statistic. The task's bar (0 good and ≥8/12) is not approached.

The **per-pixel difference**, the task's candidate #2, is **not reconstructible from this
record**: the five evidence files are the live frame, the mask, the per-cell `|d|`, the
per-cell tolerance and the metadata; the reference frame is not among them. The reference's
fingerprint (`ref_fp = 0x6792A805`) is in every `meta.json`, but a search of all 400 files of
the exact 2,764,800-byte frame size in the tree found **0** with that fingerprint
([`reference_search.txt`](shape_scan/reference_search.txt)). The cell-level `deep` grid is the
only shape of the difference the record contains, and it is negative. The per-pixel variant is
therefore **unmeasured**, not disproved; it needs the reference frame dumped with the evidence
and a bench run. No firmware change is recommended.

## 2. Pre-registration, and what it cost

The pre-registration fixed, before the formal per-board computation:

- the exact tap arithmetic (four 3×3 taps at (2,2), (6,2), (2,6), (6,6), green, `/9`, `max-min`);
- the S2 aggregation (largest 8-connected component of `fine_deep > fine_tol`, PCA elongation);
- the S3 aggregation (top-hat disk r=5, `T=20`, area ≥50, max component length);
- the acceptance: **0 good above the threshold and ≥8/12 scratches**, LOTO ≥6/12 with
  ≤1/40 held-out good;
- the null-first order and the hash guard.

The exploratory work that preceded it (frame viewing, colour mask, a few zoomed crops) is
disclosed in §0 of the prereg. It was used to identify the geometry, not to choose a threshold
or an aggregation. The **only** change after freezing is Amendment A1's denominator (40 good
frames, not 41), made after the frame inventory and before the scratch side was computed.

The 12-scratch sample is small. The AUC confidence intervals below are bootstrap 95%
intervals, 2,000 resamples, seed 20261029; they are wide by construction. A 0/12 at the 0-good
point is decisive for the tested statistic regardless of the interval; a "near-miss" AUC would
not have been.

## 3. Known-answer checks (the reader is the first instrument)

1. **Exact 3×3 sums.** `shape_scan.py`'s `box3()` was checked against a direct sum on a
   random array at interior and border points. The four taps are the pixels the firmware's
   `cell_mean8_ex()` reads; a one-pixel shift would move the S1 values by tens of grey.
2. **The deep grid is the firmware's own.** The largest 8-connected group of
   `fine_deep > fine_tol` equals the **snapshot** DAREA in `meta.json` on **78/78** frame sets
   (40 good, 38 defective). This is the same known-answer check the `amp_scan` study used; it
   passes exactly, so the grid reader and the threshold rule are not the source of the negative.
3. **The reference is absent.** Every `meta.json` carries `ref_fp = 0x6792A805`; the firmware
   computes it from the reference store with `ref_fingerprint()` (FNV-1a over every 1024th
   byte, `main.c:4323`). Searching all 400 files of exactly 2,764,800 bytes under the
   repository found **0** matches. A second reference exists only if it is outside the tree or
   was never written; neither is in this record.
4. **The row baseline is real.** The CSV's `diff_lim_used` is 6 on every row; the good g14
   row (7, `diff_verdict=1`) is a genuine false NG at that limit, not a missing value.

## 4. Statistic S1 — four-tap disagreement (live frame)

**Definition.** For each 8×8 cell, the four taps at (2,2), (6,2), (2,6), (6,6) inside the cell,
each the mean of a 3×3 green box, integer-divided by 9; the cell's spread is
`max(tap) - min(tap)`. The per-board value is the **maximum spread over the judged cells**
(`mask != 0`). This is the cheapest candidate because the firmware already reads these taps.

**Good-side null (40 boards).** min 112, q25 123.75, median 132.5, q75 140, max 165 (g20);
mean 131.9. The spread is a **texture** measure: a good board's specular/printed surface
already produces 112–165 grey between its own taps.

**Scratch side (12).** values sorted: 97, 112, 116, 124, 125, 128, 131, 132, 134, 134, 134,
149 (max d32); median 129.5, mean 126.3. The scratches are **lower** than the good boards on
average.

**ROC / table** (`numbers.txt`; full values in `table.csv`):

| threshold | good above /40 | scratch above /12 | FPR | TPR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 110 | 40 | 11 | 1.000 | 0.917 |
| 120 | 33 | 9 | 0.825 | 0.750 |
| 130 | 22 | 6 | 0.550 | 0.500 |
| 140 | 9 | 1 | 0.225 | 0.083 |
| 150 | 2 | 0 | 0.050 | 0.000 |
| 160 | 1 | 0 | 0.025 | 0.000 |
| 165 + ε | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

**AUC 0.3927** (95% CI 0.223–0.574). The AUC is below 0.5: the statistic is slightly better at
identifying **good** boards than scratches. **0-good point: 0/12.** LOTO: 0/12 detection,
1/40 held-out good above the threshold. Secondary counts/groups peak at 2/12 with 0 good
(`s1_group_10`, good max 197) and are not a shape signal.

**Verdict S1: does not separate.** By 0/12 at 0 good and by an AUC below chance.

## 5. Statistic S2 — connected-component geometry of the difference

### 5.1 Why the per-pixel version is not in this record

The task's candidate #2 asks for the **per-pixel** difference's connected components. That
operand is missing (§1.2). A surrogate reference (another good board) requires a registration
the record does not contain: `meta.json` publishes `reg_scale` but not `dx16`, `dy16` or the
angle, and `dl/twostage/reg_all.json` is the 2026-10-27 session's 40-entry transform table, not
this one. Re-fitting a transform from the live frame alone would put a registration error into
the very residual whose shape is being measured.

The shape of the difference that the record **does** contain is the per-cell `deep` grid
thresholded by the firmware's own `fine_tol`. S2 is defined on it, at the 8×8 cell geometry:

- `deep = (fine_deep > fine_tol) & (mask != 0)`;
- the largest 8-connected component's PCA elongation `sqrt(λ1/λ2)`; 0 for a one-cell
  component;
- secondary: area (must reproduce the snapshot DAREA), bbox length/width, fill, the
  all-deep-cell line support (max cells within 0.75 cell of a line through two deep cells,
  divided by the number of deep cells), and the span of that line.

### 5.2 The largest component on a scratch board is often not the scratch

On d30 the deep grid has 29 cells in 18 components. The **largest** is a compact 6-cell blob at
cells (47–49, 54–56), elongation 1.15; the **scratch** is the 5-cell line at (45–46, 73–76),
elongation 3.52 — the second component. The largest-component statistic therefore measures a
specular blob, not the defect. d23's largest component is a 5-cell group (elongation 2.53) and
no component exceeds the good maximum. The good boards' largest groups are compact 5-cell
blobs (g11 elongation 3.40, g26 1.73). The details are in
[`component_detail.txt`](shape_scan/component_detail.txt).

### 5.3 Numbers

**Good-side null (40).** Largest-component elongation: 32 boards 0 (one cell), 4 boards
1.73–1.99, 2 boards 3.40, 2 boards 4.391 (g14, g40). Median 0, mean 0.563, max 4.391.

**Scratch side (12).** 8 boards 0 (one cell), 3 boards 1.15–1.62, 1 board 2.525 (d23). Median 0,
mean 0.565, max 2.525. The scratch distribution is **inside** the good one.

| threshold | good above /40 | scratch above /12 | FPR | TPR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 8 | 4 | 0.200 | 0.333 |
| 1.0 | 8 | 4 | 0.200 | 0.333 |
| 1.5 | 8 | 2 | 0.200 | 0.167 |
| 2.0 | 4 | 1 | 0.100 | 0.083 |
| 2.5 | 4 | 1 | 0.100 | 0.083 |
| 3.0 | 4 | 0 | 0.100 | 0.000 |
| 4.0 | 2 | 0 | 0.050 | 0.000 |
| 4.391 + ε | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

**AUC 0.5417** (95% CI 0.408–0.688) — a coin flip with a wide interval. **0-good point: 0/12.**
LOTO: 0/12 detection, 0/40 held-out good. The best 0-good point over the **area** (frame
DAREA) is `>5`: 2/12 (d30, d32), 0/40 good; the best over the all-deep-cell support is 0/12
(good max 11, scratch max 10). The post-hoc "max elongation over **all** components" (not the

the pre-registered aggregation. See [`posthoc.txt`](shape_scan/posthoc.txt).

**Verdict S2: does not separate.** The good boards' 5-cell groups are already compact-to-
elongated lines; the scratch's deep cells are isolated; the largest component is often a
specular blob, not the scratch. By 0/12 at 0 good.

## 8. The decisive question, and by how much

The task's question: *is there any threshold with zero good boards above it and most scratches
above it?*

**No.** Precisely:

| statistic | 0-good threshold | scratch above | good above | AUC [95% CI] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 max tap spread | >165 | **0/12** | 0/40 | 0.393 [0.223, 0.574] |
| S2 largest-component elongation | >4.391 | **0/12** | 0/40 | 0.542 [0.408, 0.688] |
| S3 top-hat max length | >36.467 | **0/12** | 0/40 | 0.387 [0.287, 0.506] |

The best 0-good detection on **any** secondary aggregator is **2/12**: the frame DAREA rule at
`>5` (d30 and d32), and the S1 group at T=10 (which is a texture count, not shape). The
pre-registered bar was 0 good and **≥8/12**. The gap is not marginal: 0 vs 8 on the primaries,
2 vs 8 at the best secondary. The scratch distributions are not merely overlapping the good
ones; on S1 and S3 they are shifted to the **good** side of the good median, and on S2 the
scratch maximum is below the good maximum.

**By how much** (differences of the distributions):

- S1: scratch mean 126.3 vs good 131.9; scratch max 149 vs good max 165; the best scratch is
  below the best good.
- S2: scratch max 2.53 vs good max 4.39; the scratch's largest component is smaller and less
  elongated than the good's best 3-cell lines.
- S3: scratch max 29.6 vs good max 36.5; 13 good boards have a ≥5 px component, only 1 scratch
  does.

**Sample size.** The 12 scratches give AUC confidence intervals roughly ±0.14; the 0/12 at the
0-good point does not depend on the interval. A future run that claimed an AUC of 0.8 on 12
scratches would still need the 0-good point to show a gap; this one does not.

## 9. Why: the mechanism, stated with the numbers

1. **The scratch is a broad, low-contrast line, not a high-contrast edge.** In the native crop,
   d30's scratch is a branched pale line ~2–5 px wide and ~50–60 px long in that crop (see the
   image below). At the pre-registered S3 setting (top-hat r=5, T=20, area ≥50 px) d30's
   component length is **0.0**: the line is visible, but it does not form a long component at
   that threshold. The good boards' pads and design edges are the components that reach
   16–36 px; a shape detector that thresholds high enough to reject them sees the scratch as
   weak fragments, and one that thresholds low enough to keep the scratch keeps the whole
   board's texture.
2. **The cell-mean grid does not keep the line connected.** At the four 3×3 taps, the scratch
   crosses one or two taps per cell and often falls between them; on d30 it produces 29 deep
   cells in 18 components, the largest 6-cell component a compact specular blob and the scratch
   a 5-cell line. On d31 the snapshot has **0** deep cells; d24, d34 and d37 have 8, 12 and 7
   deep cells but a largest component of only 2, 2 and 1. A shape statistic cannot create
   connectivity the difference field does not contain.
3. **The good boards are not "5–7 scattered speckle".** They carry 0–33 deep cells each
   (g26 33, g20 20), with compact 5-cell blobs of 43–106 grey (g11's 5-cell group reads
   103/105/106; g26's 44–96). The task's "5–7" is the **largest group**, not the total signal.
   The good population already contains the compact blobs and the 3-cell lines that the shape
   statistic is looking for.
4. **The four-tap spread is texture, not defect.** Good boards read 112–165 grey; the scratch's
   contribution 17–50 grey is smaller than the board-to-board texture
   (`dl/rangescan/REPORT_20261029_rangescan.md` §7). This is the same result `dl/rangescan` found
   on the 2026-10-27 population; the 2026-10-29 frames reproduce it.
5. **The per-pixel version is not in the record.** Even if the per-pixel line were continuous,
   the reference frame is absent. A surrogate reference adds a registration error larger than
   the scratch signal: the project's own single-frame replay reports a mean |d| **1.4–2.4×**
   the firmware's on every good board, i.e. the replay is noisier, never quieter
   (`dl/rangescan/REPORT_20261029_rangescan.md` §3.1). The scratch's pixel contrast is of the
   same order. A per-pixel shape statistic would need the actual reference and the actual
   per-board transform, neither of which is stored.

Native-pixel evidence (same crop coordinates on both boards):

![d30 scratch: branched pale line on the green solder mask](shape_scan/view/d30_line.png)

![g0, same crop: no such line](shape_scan/view/g0_line.png)

The scratch is visible to the eye; it is not a strong, straight, high-contrast line that a
cell-mean or a top-hat can isolate from the product's own texture.

## 10. What would be needed to test the per-pixel candidate, and what it would cost

The only unmeasured candidate is the **per-pixel** connected-component/ridge statistic. To test
it properly:

1. Dump the reference store (`REF_BASE`, 2,764,800 bytes) with the evidence set, or add it to
   the `EVID_*` block (2.76 MB more DDR, or another static region; the exact placement is a
   design decision). This is a PS-only firmware change and a new ELF.
2. Dump the per-board transform (`dx16`, `dy16`, angle, scale) next to the frame; the firmware
   already has these words.
3. Re-run the **same** pre-registered per-pixel S2: register with the dumped transform, form
   `live - reference` after the firmware's gain/bias, threshold at the same tolerance, and
   measure the largest/all-component elongation. No new threshold search.
4. Cost: a PS-only build (no PL re-synthesis) + ELF reload + a new reference capture + the 12
   scratch boards placed again. The reference take clears the tolerance map and shifts the
   whole DAREA column (chapter 14), so the 12 boards must be re-captured; the operator cost is
   the same order as a sweep (tens of minutes plus setup). The evidence copy and the host dump
   each grow by 2.76 MB; the JTAG dump is the slow part.

This is a **measurement enabler**, not a recommended product change. Given the cell-level and
live-frame negatives, the prior is that the per-pixel line will also be dominated by the
good-board texture; but the record cannot settle it.

### The word range: the task's premise is stale

The task says 583..639 is the only range free in both namespaces. In the current worktree that
is no longer true:

- `dl/mb_free.py` reports the only common free block as **588..639 (52 words)** (it also
  reports 572..574 free, but 583..587 are now used).
- `dl/host_qc/qc_mailbox.py` uses **583** `part_rulesel`, **584** `part_chroma`, **585**
  `frz_scale`, **586** `frz_bias`, **587** `frz_state`; its highest index is 587.

The common free block is therefore **588..639**, not 583..639. Since this study recommends no
firmware change, no words are reserved; the correction is recorded so a future change does not
collide with the mask-freeze words. See
[`word_range.txt`](shape_scan/word_range.txt).

No firmware change is proposed: the statistic that would justify it is not there.

**If it had worked, what it would have cost.** S1 is the cheapest by construction: the four
taps are already read in `cell_mean8_ex()`, so a per-board max spread needs one register and
one mailbox word (588..639) and **no extra memory traffic**; it is the only candidate that
could be called a minimal change. S2 needs the deep grid (already computed) and one more
pass over it, still PS-only; S3 needs a per-pixel top-hat and is the most expensive of the
three. The measured AUCs/detections do not justify any of them, so the honest
recommendation is no change, and no word is reserved.

## 12. Not measured, and what remains uncertain

- **Per-pixel difference shape (candidate #2 in its literal form):** not measured, because the
  reference frame is not in the record (0/400 frames with `ref_fp`). The cell-level `deep` grid
  is the proxy; it is negative. The per-pixel variant is the one thing a bench run with the
  reference dumped could still change.
- **The actual reference's gain/bias and transform:** not in `meta.json`; `reg_scale` is the
  only transform word stored. The 2026-10-29 session has no `reg_all.json`.
- **The 41st good board (g38):** no frame; the null is 40. The task's 41-good row population
  includes g20 twice and excludes g3 (no DAREA); the frame null is 40 distinct labels.
- **The snapshot vs row comparison:** the frames are the second comparison; d31's snapshot has
  0 deep cells, so the cell-level statistic cannot see the board the row called DAREA 2. This
  is repeatability, not shape.
- **The scratch's physical width/contrast on all 12 boards:** only d30 was inspected at native
  resolution. The other scratches are represented by their deep grids and top-hat responses;
  no per-board physical measurement was made.
- **A production threshold:** none is proposed. The negative is at the statistic level; it says
  do not spend a build on this family.

## 13. 中文摘要

**结论：不成立，而且不是差一点。** 三个预注册的形状统计量在 40 块良品与 12 块划伤板之间都不分
离：四抽头极差 AUC 0.393（良品 112–165，划伤 97–149，划伤反而更低）；最大连通块长宽比

0–29.6）。在"零良品超过阈值"的点上，三个统计量的检出都是 **0/12**；任何次要聚合的最好
0 误检点是 **2/12**（还是帧 DAREA 规则，不是形状）。离"0 良品 + ≥8/12"很远。

**逐像素差值无法从这份记录复算。** 五个证据文件是活帧、掩模、逐格 `|d|`、逐格容差和元数据；

## 15. Bottom line for the owner

- **Do not implement the tap-disagreement term, the cell-shape term or the live-frame ridge
  term.** Each is measured here; each is at or below chance for this class.
- **Do not spend a build on the cell-level shape family.** The scratch is inside the good
  distribution on every statistic; the good boards are the texture-rich population, not the
  scratches.
- **The only open question is the literal per-pixel difference**, and it is open for a record
  reason (no reference frame), not for a statistical one. If it is ever worth a bench run, the
  measurement is: add the reference and the transform to the evidence, re-capture the 12
  scratch boards, and apply the same pre-registered S2 at the pixel scale. Until then, the
  honest statement is: **the shape statistic does not separate the class on this record; the
  best 0-good detection is 2/12, and the pre-registered primaries are 0/12.**
