## Stage 2: shipped, and what it really did

N = 8 frames, only while `MB_READY` = 1 and only frames the tear detector did not flag,
presented to both the registration (the lattice) and the comparison (the 16x16 cell means).
137 independent comparisons at N = 8: **cells 0 in 135, 1 in two, none at or above the 8-cell
limit** (at N = 1: 0 in 36 of 43, 8..9 in two). sigma of the cell difference 7.2 → 4.4 grey
counts. Cells over the threshold before the blob rule: 40 (median) → 0. Loop time unchanged
(156.55 ms at N = 1, 4 and 8, measured back to back on one scene) — the work is hidden by a
wait, not free.

**The finding that matters more than the win**: averaging stopped HIDING a registration
drift. On the shipped build, a drift to −0.20 deg / −3.5 px was followed by cells = 0 with
193 cells over the threshold — the noise scattered them and the 3x3 blob rule removed them.
The firmware therefore reports PASS on a part it has just mis-registered. At N = 8 the same
drift leaves a clustered 14..25-cell difference. Drift was seen in 2 of 3 runs at N = 4
(before the tear gate) and 0 of 2 at N = 8, so the N = 8 rate is unresolved — item 1 of the
Stage 2 document has the repeat experiment (5 runs, count `MB_REG_MODE` != 2).

The row profile of the difference (`MB_DIFF_ROW`, 45 words) on a still scene is a smooth
±5-count periodic modulation with a ~12.5-row period, 3.6 cycles down the frame: the
rolling-shutter/mains bar as a spatial structure, below the 14-count threshold.

## State of the bench, stated plainly

The scene was NOT still for parts of this session: the firmware's own gate declined 25..73
loops in several runs, and one acceptance attempt read 20 of 49 comparisons over the limit,
all in a box at the frame's LEFT EDGE (not the part). Two runs of this session (a Stage 2
drift and the Stage 5 verification) also show the transform at −0.04..−0.30 deg with dx 0.75..4
px, held (mode 2), which is what produces the 14..64-cell readings when no fresh reference
has been taken. **Take a fresh reference (`MB_CMD = 9`) before quoting any cell count**, and
check `MB_AVG_SKIP` did not move during the run.
