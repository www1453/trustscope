## 0. The one-paragraph answer

**No — and the ninth attempt cannot be a new test of anything.** The task's statistic ("the
largest connected component's cell count" of the ≥30-grey amplitude mask) **is the firmware's
verdict word**, `MB_DIFF_DAREA` (`ps_ui/main.c:2727`, "THE AUTHORITY: largest connected deep
group, cells"), computed every comparison at `main.c:10182` and already the operand of the
verdict. Its zero-good ceiling was measured and published in the previous round: **2/12,
AUC 0.600** (`dl/SHAPE_20261029.md` §6, commit `957153b`). This study reproduces it to the
board — good maximum 5 at `g11/g15/g26`, scratch maximum 6 at `d30/d32`, **2/12 at 0/40, AUC
0.6000 [0.408, 0.783]** — with an independent reader that passes 78/78 DAREA identities. The
only part the earlier rounds did not test, the **structure** (one connected run vs scattered
singles), is also negative, and it fails in the **reverse** direction: both classes are ~85%
single cells (good 207/243 components, scratch 76/90); the scratch is **not** one run (on
`d30` — the one board whose line was located independently — 29 deep cells fall in 18
components, the largest a 6-cell patch at cells (x,y) (47,54)–(49,56), 17 rows from the line at
(45–46, 73–76)); and the good boards' "5–7" **is** a connected patch by definition of the
statistic (each of `g11`, `g15`, `g26` carries one 5-cell component). The pre-registered share
`S = L/DN` is bounded by 1, two good boards (each with exactly **one** deep cell) attain 1, so
its zero-good point lies **outside the statistic's range**: it detects **0/12 scratch and 0/21
contamination** — the pure "loses every contamination detection and gains nothing" case the
task warned about. The bar (0 good and ≥8/12) is not approached: the ceiling is 2/12.

## 1. What the task asked for, and why the primary is not a new statistic

The task asks for the per-cell amplitude mask exactly as the firmware builds it, the connected
components, and the **largest component's cell count** as the board's statistic. The shipped
source says what that is:

| fact | source |
|---|---|
| `MB_DIFF_DAREA 23` = "THE AUTHORITY: largest connected deep group, cells" | `ps_ui/main.c:2727` |
| the flag grid is set when `fad > tolv`, `tolv` floored at `DEFECT_AMP` | `main.c:10138`, `10154` |
| `DEFECT_AMP 30` ("grey levels: \|d\| above this is deep"), floor `TOL_FLOOR = DEFECT_AMP` | `main.c:2884`, `2890` |
| `darea = cc_largest(PART_CC_TMP)` | `main.c:10182` |
| `cc_largest`: largest **8-connected** component, explicit BFS, mark at push | `main.c:6249-6314` |
| the firmware's own area gate: `DEFECT_AREA = DIFF_LIM_DEFAULT`, "0..7 pass, 8+ fail" | `main.c:2885`, `1800` |
| the host re-checks the same rule at capture time: "largest 8-connected group of cells where deep > tol" | `dl/host_qc/evidence.py:213-251` |

So "the largest component's cell count" **is** the area the verdict already reads. The task's
premise — that the earlier rounds tested only the *elongation* of that component and not its
*size* — is not what the record says: the earlier round's §6 table carries a row labelled
"record snapshot DAREA", giving the good maximum 5 (`g11, g15, g26`), the scratch maximum 6
(`d30, d32`), the 0-good detection **2/12** and the AUC **0.600**. That is this statistic,
already measured.

This study therefore does three things, and says which is which:

1. **Re-derives the primary** from the raw per-cell grids with an independent reader, as a
   known-answer control. **This cannot produce a new negative** — it reproduces the published
   one. It does confirm that the reader, the threshold and the connectivity are right.
2. **Tests the one genuinely untested statistic**: the structure of the deep cells, `S = L/DN`.
   No per-board value of `DN`, of the component count, of the component-size histogram, or of
   `S` was computed or published before this study's frozen pre-registration.
3. **Measures the contamination/corner cost** at the same operating point, because a scratch
   gained against a contamination lost is not a win.

The amplitude threshold the task asks to verify is indeed **30 grey** (`DEFECT_AMP`), the
comparison is **strict `>`** (the firmware's `fad > tolv`), and the connectivity is
**8-connected** (`cc_largest`). None of the three was guessed; each is quoted above.

## 2. The measurement, exactly

- **Grid**: 160×90 cells, row-major, `fi = fy*160 + fx` (`FINE_W`, `FINE_H`, `main.c:1946`).
- **Amplitude**: `deep = fine_deep.bin` (per-cell `|d|`, clamped to 255, **0 for an unjudged
  cell**); `tol = fine_tol.bin` (the tolerance that judged the cell).
- **Mask**: `deep > tol` (strict). A cell that was not judged has `deep = 0`, which is never
  `> tol`, so it breaks a group exactly as in the firmware. The redundant `mask != 0` condition
  was computed as well and agrees on **78/78** sets.
- **Threshold**: the tolerance map is the floor everywhere — min = max = 30, cells with
  `tol != 30`: **0**, `tol_exact = 1` on every set — so the mask is `|d| > 30`, the firmware's
  `DEFECT_AMP`.
- **Components**: 8-connectivity, the firmware's `cc_largest` rule. Two independent
  implementations were run on every board (scipy's 8-connected labelling and a
  pure-Python BFS copying `cc_largest`); they agree on every board.
- **The operator matters on 107 cells.** **107 cells across the 78 boards read exactly 30.**
  The firmware's `>` excludes them. The task's prose says "≥"; a post-hoc sensitivity
  ([`sensitivity_ge.py`](connarea_20261030/sensitivity_ge.py),
  [`sensitivity_ge.txt`](connarea_20261030/sensitivity_ge.txt)) recomputes the same statistic
  with `>=`: **2 of 52** boards change (`d28` 1→2, `d30` 6→7), the good maximum stays **5**, the
  0-good detection stays **2/12** and the AUC moves 0.6000 → 0.6292. **The verdict is the same
  under either reading of the operator**, so this ambiguity cannot rescue the statistic.

### 2.1 Known-answer checks (the reader is the first instrument)

| check | expectation | result |
|---|---|---|
| R1 file sizes | mask/deep/tol 14,400; frame 2,764,800 | 78/78 |
| R2 **DAREA identity** | largest 8-connected group of `deep > tol` == `meta.snapshot.darea` | **78/78** |
| R3 component bookkeeping | `sum(sizes) == DN`, `max(sizes) == L` | 78/78 |
| R4 two implementations | scipy `L` == BFS `L` (the firmware's algorithm) | **78/78** |
| R5 mask condition | `(deep > tol)` == `(deep > tol) & (mask != 0)` | 78/78 |
| R6 tolerance map | min = max = 30, `tol != 30` cells 0, `tol_exact = 1` | 78/78 |
| R7 boundary | cells with `deep == 30` | **107** (counted, §2) |

R2 is the decisive one: it is the same identity the host checks before a snapshot may enter the
frame set, and it passes on every board. The negative is therefore not a reader failure.

## 5. Secondary S — scattered singles vs one connected run (the one new test)

### 5.1 Definition and the null

`S = L / DN`, the largest component's share of the board's deep cells. `S = 1` means every deep
cell is in the single largest component ("one run"); `S ≈ 1/DN` means the cells are all
isolated. Boards with `DN = 0` are undefined (−1.0, never detected, ranked last) — 3 good,
1 scratch.

**Good-side null (37 of 40 defined):** min 0.1250, q25 0.1818, **median 0.2000**, q75 0.3333,
**max 1.0000**, mean 0.3062. **`S = 1` on 2 of 40** — `g5` and `g31`, and **both have exactly
one deep cell** (L = 1, DN = 1).

**Scratch side (11 of 12 defined):** min 0.1429, q25 0.1868, **median 0.2500**, q75 0.3667,
**max 1.0000**, mean 0.3269. **`S = 1` on 1 of 12** — `d27`, again **one deep cell**.

### 5.2 What `S = 1` actually means on this population

It does **not** mean "a connected run of 1–7 cells". It means **"this board has exactly one deep
cell"**. Every board whose largest component is 5–7 — the good `g11`, `g15`, `g26` and the
scratch `d23`, `d29`, `d30`, `d32` — has `S < 0.46`, because the rest of its deep cells are
elsewhere. The statistic conflates the only structurally trivial case (one cell) with the case
the task hypothesised (one run).

### 5.3 ROC, AUC and the verdict

| threshold S > | good above /40 | scratch above /12 |
|---|---|---|
| **1.0000** | **0** | **0** |
| 0.5000 | 2 | 1 (d27) |
| 0.4545 | 6 | 1 |
| 0.4167 | 7 | 1 |
| 0.4000 | 7 | 2 |
| 0.3846 | 7 | 3 |
| 0.3333 | 8 | 3 |

**0-good point: S > 1.0 → 0/12.** But `S ≤ 1` by construction, so this threshold is **outside
the statistic's range**: it is not a discriminating point, it is the top of the scale. At the
next achievable threshold (> 0.5) the statistic accepts **2 false positives** and still detects
**1/12** — and that one board is `d27`, a single cell, i.e. the one scratch with no structure at
all. AUC **0.5307** (95% CI 0.337–0.713) — a coin flip. LOTO: 0/12, 0/40.

**Verdict: does not separate — 0/12 against a bar of 8/12, and the closest it comes is 1/12 with
2 good boards false.**

### 5.4 The conditional view (descriptive, not the verdict)

On the boards that have the structure to speak of (`DN ≥ 2`: 35 good, 10 scratch), the
direction is **reversed**: good `S` = 0.1250–0.5000 (median 0.2000, C median 6), scratch `S` =
0.1429–0.4167 (median 0.2386, C median 7). **The good side's maximum share (0.5000) is above
the scratch's (0.4167).** This is the same direction the previous round found for elongation:
the good boards, not the scratches, are the more connected population. It is reported to
explain the negative; it was not the pre-registered statistic and it is not used as a win.

## 6. The risk the task asked to test: are the good boards' 5–7 cells a connected patch?

**Yes — by definition, and the data shows it.** `L` *is* the size of the largest component, so a
good board reading 5 has a connected 5-cell patch. On the frame:

| board | L | DN | C | component-size histogram | L bbox (x0,y0)-(x1,y1) | L peak (grey) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| g11 | 5 | 11 | 7 | `1:6 5:1` | (64,62)–(65,65) | 106 |
| g15 | 5 | 13 | 9 | `1:8 5:1` | (86,71)–(89,72) | 62 |
| g26 | 5 | 33 | 20 | `1:12 2:5 3:2 5:1` | (63,55)–(65,56) | 96 |

So the good boards' "5 cells" is **one compact patch plus scattered speckle**, exactly the
structure the task expected the scratch to have. There is no good board whose five cells are
five scattered singles: for such a board `L` would be 1, not 5.

**And the scratch is not one run either.** The two largest scratch components are:

| d32 | 6 | 15 | 7 | `1:3 2:3 6:1` | (67,56)–(69,58) | 84 | not independently located |

On `d30` the largest component is **not the scratch**: it is a 6-cell patch at **rows 54–56,
columns 47–49**, whose nearest cell is **17 rows and 1 column** from the line's nearest cell,
while the line the previous study located at native resolution spans **rows 73–76, columns
45–46**. The line itself contributes isolated cells and a 5-cell fragment (`1:15 3:1 5:1 6:1` —
15 singletons). This reproduces, at the size level, what `SHAPE` §5.2 measured at the elongation

## 7. The contamination and corner cost at the same operating point

The task's rule: a statistic that loses a contamination detection to gain a scratch is not a
win. At the statistic's 0-good point and at the station's own points:

| rule | good | scratch | contamination | corner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| row DAREA ≥ 6 (this sweep's limit) | **1/40** (g14) | 1/12 (d30) | 18/21 | 0/2 |
| row DAREA ≥ 8 (firmware `DIFF_LIM_DEFAULT`) | 0/40 | 0/12 | 16/21 | 0/2 |
| row DAREA > 7 (row 0-good point) | 0/40 | 0/12 | 16/21 | 0/2 |
| frame L ≥ 8 (firmware default) | 0/40 | 0/12 | 16/21 | 0/2 |
| **frame L ≥ 6, i.e. L > 5 (this statistic's 0-good point)** | **0/40** | **2/12** | **19/21** | **0/2** |
| frame S > 1.0 (S's 0-good point) | 0/40 | 0/12 | **0/21** | 0/2 |

**The primary does not pay the contamination price — because it is the contamination
detector.** At its 0-good point it reads 19/21 contamination (18/21 at the station's row limit
6), and the scratch gain is 2/12, not 8/12. The two contamination misses at that point are
`d7` (L = 5) and `d13` (L = 3), both inside the good null; the third row miss `d9` reads 7 on
the frame and is caught.

**The structural secondary pays the maximum price.** At its 0-good point it detects **0/21
contamination, 0/2 corner and 0/12 scratch**: it has lost every contamination detection and
gained nothing. With the numbers: contamination L is 3–43 (median 15), scratch L is 0–6
(median 2), good L is 0–5 (median 1). The area statistic is a contamination detector; the
structure statistic is not a detector of anything at 0 false positives.

**Corner**: `d38` L = 1, `d39` L = 3. No threshold in this study detects a corner board, and
the firmware's own note already records corner chips at DAREA 4, 6, 5, 0/3 caught
(`main.c:2565`). The ninth attempt does not change that.

## 8. Where the "2/12" comes from — and why it is not a gain

The only number in this study that looks better than the shipped rule is the primary's **2/12 at
0/40** versus the row's **0/12**. It is not a better statistic and not a new operating point. It
is the **same rule on a second comparison**, and the null moved:

| board | row DAREA | frame L | difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| **g14** (the row's only 7) | **7** | **3** | −4 |
| d32 | 3 | **6** | +3 |
| d31 | 2 | 0 | −2 |
| d29 | 4 | 5 | +1 |
| d30 | 7 | 6 | −1 |

The row's 0-good point is `> 7` because `g14` read 7; the frame's is `> 5` because `g14` read 3.
At `> 7` the row detects **0/12** scratches; at `> 5` the frame detects **2/12** (`d30` is
already caught at limit 6, so the net new board is `d32`). The firmware's own comment measures
exactly this: "a stationary board's `MB_DIFF_DAREA` moves by **±6 cells** between comparisons,
and the 41 good boards of the 2026-10-29 sweep read 0..7 with median 2" (`main.c:2408-2410`).
A 2-cell move on one board is inside that, and it is the whole of the apparent improvement.
**The ninth attempt's 2/12 is a null fluctuation, not a detection.** The bar is 8/12, six
boards away.

## 9. Verdict

| statistic | good null | scratch null | 0-good threshold | scratch above | AUC [95% CI] | bar | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **L** (largest component) | 0–5, median 1 | 0–6, median 2 | > 5 | **2/12** | **0.600** [0.408, 0.783] | 8/12 | **FAIL** |
| **S** (L/DN, structure) | 0.125–1.0, median 0.2 | 0.143–1.0, median 0.25 | > 1.0 | **0/12** | **0.531** [0.337, 0.713] | 8/12 | **FAIL** |

**Works: NO.** The pre-registered criterion (0/40 good and ≥8/12 scratch) is not met by either
statistic; the LOTO check (≥6/12 with ≤1/40) is not met either (L 2/12, S 0/12). The primary is
a re-derivation of the published 2/12 and AUC 0.600; the secondary is a new negative and it is
quantitatively worse.

### What this closes

1. **The "largest connected component's size" family.** It is not unexplored: it is
   `MB_DIFF_DAREA`, the verdict word, and its zero-good ceiling is 2/12 (AUC 0.600), measured
   here to the board and published in the previous round. No threshold on it reaches 8/12,
   because the scratch class sits at 0–6 and the good null reaches 5 — the 0-good point is
   above the **entire good null** and above **all but two** scratches.
2. **The scattered-vs-one-run structure hypothesis.** It is false in both halves: the scratch
   is not one run (d30's line is fragmented; its largest component is a 6-cell patch away from
   the line), and the good boards' 5-cell counts *are* connected patches. Both populations are
   ~85% single cells; the scratch's component-size histogram is a near-copy of the good one;
   the contamination's is a different object. The area statistic is a contamination detector,
   not a scratch detector.
3. **The "the gate is the obstacle" framing.** The gate *is* the statistic. Measuring the gate's
   own statistic at the gate's own threshold returns what the gate already returns. The ninth
   attempt cannot be a win by construction; the only new question was the structure, and it is
   negative.

### What it does not claim

- Not that no statistic can work — only that this one, and the structure of the deep cells it
  is built on, do not.
- Not the per-pixel difference (the reference is not in the record; the previous round closed
  that family with 0/12) and not the B channel (one board, post-hoc).
- Not a production threshold. None is proposed.

## 10. If it had separated: the minimal change, and what a bench confirmation needs

It did not separate, so **no firmware change is proposed.** For completeness, the conditional
answer is unusually cheap: the clustering already exists and already runs every comparison
(`cc_largest`, `main.c:6255`; `MB_DIFF_DAREA` is published at `main.c:10249` with its amplitude,
threshold and bbox). A separating result would need **no new loop, no new DDR, no PL work** —
only the verdict rule or the area limit (`DEFECT_AREA` / `MB_DIFF_LIM`, one word) would change.
The cost of that change is exactly §7: at the firmware's own gate 8, contamination 16/21 and
scratch 0/12; at the tighter limit 6, contamination 18/21 and scratch 1/12 on the row. The
limit is the only knob, and it trades the two classes against each other.

A bench confirmation would be: re-take the reference (`MB_CMD = 9`), re-run ≥40 good boards and
the same 12 scratch boards, capture the same five evidence files per board, and run
`connarea.py --all` unchanged with the same acceptance. Do not mix rows across references: a
reference take clears the tolerance map and shifts the whole DAREA column. The expected outcome
from this measurement is **2/12 again**; a bench run claiming ≥8/12 would contradict 52
registered comparisons and would need an explanation before it was believed.

## 11. Reproduction

One command, measured wall time **0.85 s** on the final run (0.40 s on a warm re-run; 2.74 s on
the first cold run; the null alone was 1.10 s cold):

```
python dl\connarea_20261030\connarea.py --all
```

It verifies the pre-registration's sha256 first, computes the 40-good null and hashes it, then
re-reads the null file and refuses the defect side unless the hash matches. Outputs:
[`table.csv`](connarea_20261030/table.csv) (one row per board), [`results.json`](connarea_20261030/results.json)
(per-board values, ROC paths, AUC/CI, cost table), [`numbers.txt`](connarea_20261030/numbers.txt)
(every headline), [`histograms.txt`](connarea_20261030/histograms.txt) (component-size
histograms), [`run.txt`](connarea_20261030/run.txt) (the console transcript).

Post-hoc, clearly labelled, cannot change the verdict:

## 12. 中文摘要

**结论：不分离，而且第九次尝试在构造上就不可能是一次新检验。**

任务提出的统计量——"阈值 30 灰以上的幅度掩模里最大连通块的格数"——**就是固件判据本身**：

每次比较都在 `main.c:10182` 计算，是判决的读数据。它的"零良品"上限在上一轮已经发表：

每一块板：良品最大 **5**（g11/g15/g26），划伤最大 **6**（d30/d32），**0/40 良品时 2/12**，
AUC **0.6000** [0.408, 0.783]；78/78 块板的 DAREA 恒等式通过（读取器不是原因）。

**唯一没测过的部分——结构：是"一条连通带"还是"散点"——也是负的，而且方向相反。**
两类都约 85% 是单格连通块（良品 207/243，划伤 76/90）；划伤**不是**一条连通带：d30 的 29 个
深格落在 18 个连通块里，最大的是 x 47–49、y 54–56 的 6 格块——而前一轮目视定位的划痕在
x 45–46、y 73–76（相距 17 行），**最大连通块不是划痕**。良品的"5–7"按定义**就是**连通块

检出、什么也没换到"的最坏情形。AUC 0.531 [0.337, 0.713]。

**代价。** 原统计量在零良品点（帧 L>5）上：污染 **19/21**、角缺 0/2、划伤 2/12；固件默认
门限 8 上：污染 16/21、划伤 0/12；按行（判决真正用的数）门限 6：污染 18/21、划伤 1/12、良品
1/40 误判（g14）。所以它**没有**用污染换划伤——因为它本来就是污染检测器；它只是检不出划伤。
结构统计量则把 21 块污染全部丢掉。

±6 格**（`main.c:2408-2410`）。2 格的变化全在噪声内，离 8/12 还差 6 块板。

## 14. Bottom line for the owner

- **The ninth candidate is not a candidate.** "Largest connected component's cell count" is
  `MB_DIFF_DAREA`, the word the verdict already reads; its 0-good ceiling is **2/12 (AUC 0.600)**
  and this is the second independent measurement of it, not a new one.
- **The structure hypothesis is dead in both directions.** The scratch is not one connected run
  (its largest component is a 5–6-cell patch, on d30 demonstrably not the line); the good
  boards' 5-cell counts *are* connected patches. Both sides are ~85% single cells. The
  contamination is the class with the large components (up to 43 cells), and the area statistic
  is a contamination detector.
- **The contamination cost is not a scratch gain anywhere.** At the primary's 0-good point the
  statistic still catches 19/21 contamination and 2/12 scratch; the structural statistic
  catches 0/21 contamination and 0/12 scratch. Neither meets 0 good + ≥8/12.
- **Do not spend a build on this family.** The statistic already exists; the limit is the only
  knob, and the measured trade is in §7. The honest statement is: **the class is not separable
  by the cell-area statistic, and the "one run" structure it was hoped to exploit is not there
  in either population.**
