# The full-cell (64-pixel) maximum of |live - reference|: pre-registered, measured, and it does not separate the scratch class

2026-10-29, board session and offline analysis. The board was **powered on and read once**:
one read-only xsdb session, no mailbox write, no reset, no re-take, no build, no flash, no
edit of `ps_ui/main.c`. The station was not otherwise touched.

Frozen before the dump and before any per-board number (committed first):

---

## 0. The one-paragraph answer

**No.** The pre-registered statistic `A1 = max over judged cells of (max over the cell's 64
pixels of |live - reference|)` reads **168.4 grey on the good side** (g40) and **137.0 on the
scratch side** (d32): at the only 0-good threshold, **0 of 12** scratch boards are above it,
and the AUC is **0.369** (below chance; 95% CI 0.200-0.548). The robust variant A2 (the
cell's 4th-largest pixel) is also **0/12**. The negative survives the photometric model:
with the literal `|live - reference|` it is 0/12 (AUC 0.371), with the firmware's own
mean-gain-and-bias model it is 0/12 (AUC 0.396). The mechanism is measured, not inferred:
on d30 -- the one scratch whose line was located independently, at cells (45-46, 73-76) --
the 64-pixel maximum **at the line's own cells is 58 grey**, only 1.3-1.6x the cell's mean
of the same difference, i.e. **the line is broad, not sub-tap**; the good boards reach 168
because two *different units of the same product* differ by up to ~200 grey at specular
pads. The per-pixel maximum is a unit-to-unit surface-difference meter, not a scratch
detector. The existing cell-mean rule catches 2/12 at 0 good; the full-cell maximum catches
0/12. **This closes the full-cell-maximum family.**

## 1. What was asked, and why it was the one thing still open

The previous round (`dl/SHAPE_20261029.md`, commit `957153b`) pre-registered three
cell/tap-level shape statistics and all three failed at **0/12** on the 0-good point. Its
own explanation was that a cell-mean built from four 3x3 taps cannot see a structure that
falls between the taps. The statistic that reads **all 64 pixels of the 8x8 cell** was the
only member of the family left untested, and it could not be tested on the record because
the **reference frame** was not in it (0/400 files with `ref_fp = 0x6792A805`).

The reference lives in DDR on the running board. This session dumped it **once**, verified
it by the firmware's own fingerprint, registered each stored live frame to it, and computed
the pre-registered statistic.

## 2. The reference: address, fingerprint, lattice

### 2.1 Where it is

From `ps_ui/main.c` (the running build family; `REF_BASE` unchanged since Stage 8), checked
against the whole DDR map in the same file:

| object | address | bytes | what |
|---|---|---|---|
| `REF_BASE` | `0x12400000` | 2,764,800 (`0x2A3000`, ends `0x126A3000`) | full-resolution 1280x720x3 reference, written by `ref_capture()` |
| `GREY_BASE` | `0x127C0000` | 230,400 (640x360) | the reference's half-resolution green lattice (`grey_capture()`) |
| `BASE_GM` | `0x11FA3000` | 230,400 (640x360) | its gradient-magnitude lattice (`gm_base_fill()`) |
| `BASE_MASK` | `0x126C4000` | 14,400 (160x90) | the reference's part mask (`part_masks()`) |
| `TOL_HDR` | `0x126FF840` | 5 words | the tolerance header; `TOL_HDR[4]` is the reference fingerprint |

No DMA touches any of these; the reference is written only by `ref_take()`.

### 2.2 The identification, by content

The fingerprint is **not trusted as a number in a note**. `ref_fingerprint()`
(`ps_ui/main.c:4394-4402`) is 32-bit FNV-1a (`h = 2166136261`, `h = (h ^ byte) * 16777619`
mod 2^32) over **every 1024th byte** of `REF_BASE` -- bytes 0, 1024, ..., 2,763,776, 2,700
samples. The host implementation is checked against the published FNV-1a vectors by the
same script (`b''` -> `0x811C9DC5`, `b'foobar'` -> `0xBF9CF968`; both pass).

The dump was gated on three independent words in **one xsdb session**:

```
FWID0=0x31435354 FWID1=0x43373233 FWID2=0x37393544   -> "TSC1327CD597"   gate PASS
REF_FNV=0x6792A805  TOL_HDR4=0x6792A805               (the FNV loop, 41.7 s)
GATE=PASS -> mrd -bin -file of the five regions
REF_FNV_AFTER=0x6792A805                              (no re-take in the dump window)
```

Host-side, on the dumped bytes:

| check | result |
|---|---|
| `ref.bin` size / FNV-1a stride-1024 | 2,764,800 B / **0x6792A805** = the `ref_fp` of all **79** `meta.json` files |
| `grey_capture(dumped REF)` vs dumped `GREY_BASE` | **0 mismatches** |
| `gm_base_fill(dumped GREY_BASE)` vs dumped `BASE_GM` | **0 mismatches** |
| `BASE_MASK` set cells | **685** = `part_ref` in all 79 `meta.json` |

The real-image cross-checks are the strong ones: the dumped full-resolution frame, its
640x360 grey lattice and its gradient lattice were written by the same `ref_take()` from
the same pixels, and they agree byte for byte. `dl/pixelmax_20261029/reference_check.txt`
records `REFERENCE_CHECK=PASS`.

Dumped buffer hashes (sha256, first 16 hex): ref `5b5a5ed97fa0a46e`, grey
`084b3b57eedd3969`, gm `c182c7f8b2206357`, mask `0ae9c79a2a463033`.

### 2.3 The colour lattice

The frame is fully demosaiced RGB888, **not** a Bayer mosaic: the per-parity means of each
channel differ by at most 0.36 grey (R 0.36, G 0.29, B 0.21 over the whole reference), so a
one-pixel registration error cannot create a 2x2 checkerboard artefact. Green is byte
`3i+1`, the channel every comparison sample in the firmware reads.

## 4. Registration: the transform the record does not contain

`meta.json` publishes `reg_scale` but not `dx16`, `dy16` or the angle, so each stored live
frame was registered to the dumped reference here, with the firmware's own model
(`ref_at`, `main.c:8977-9002`; `ps_ui/stD_reg_fit.py`):

```
ref_sample(x,y) = bilinear(REF, Tx, Ty)
Tx = 640 + s*(cos t*(x-640) - sin t*(y-360)) - Dx
Ty = 360 + s*(sin t*(x-640) + cos t*(y-360)) - Dy
```

on the green channel, minimising mean `|LIVE(p) - REF(T(p))|` over the live-mask cells.
Amendment A1's estimator: pyramid 1/8 -> 1/4 -> full-resolution exhaustive search over
disclosed boxes, then the frozen Nelder-Mead.

**Instrument validation (must pass before any board number).** Two synthetic pairs were
built by warping the **dumped reference** with known transforms and recovered by the same
estimator:

| true (Dx, Dy, theta, s) | recovered | error | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| (13.37, -7.71, 0.63, 0.9723) | (13.370, -7.710, 0.630, 0.972300) | < 0.001 px, < 0.001 deg, < 1e-5 | PASS |
| (-9.20, 11.40, -0.41, 1.0068) | (-9.200, 11.400, -0.410, 1.006800) | < 0.001 px, < 0.001 deg, < 1e-5 | PASS |

That validates the geometry, the signs and that the search reaches the global minimum on a
noise-free pair. It does not validate robustness to two *different* boards; the real-run
residuals below are that evidence.

**Per-board quality (all 52 boards, no exclusions):**

| quantity | range | median |
|---|---|---|
| fit residual, mean `|d|` (grey) | 2.40 .. 17.10 | 7.98 |
| residual at identity (same points) | 2.52 .. 29.19 | -- |
| fit / identity residual | 0.29 .. 0.95 | 0.43 |
| shift `|D|` (source px) | 0.15 .. 56.24 | 25.52 |
| `|theta|` (deg) | 0.01 .. 3.54 | 1.00 |
| fitted scale `s` | 0.9298 .. 1.0259 | -- |
| judged cells per board | 380 .. 647 | -- |

The registration reduces the residual by 4.6-71% (median 57%) on every board, and the
parameter gates of §4 excluded none. Per-board values are in `table.csv`.

**Two limitations, stated rather than hidden.**

1. The fitted `s` does **not** track the firmware's published `reg_scale` tightly: the
   difference is +0.019 mean, 0.028 rms, 0.072 max over the 52 boards. The firmware's own
   published transform is not a ground truth -- `dl/VERDICT_SCOPE_20261024.md` §4 already
   records a published 14.1 half-res px / 3.48 deg against an independent fit of 18.6 /
   4.68 -- and this fit lowers the mean |d| on every board, so the fit is the better
   alignment by the measure that matters here. But it is a warning, not a certificate.
2. The scale surface is multi-modal on at least one board (g20): the fit's local minimum
   (`s=0.930`, cost 16.99) is 6% from a slightly lower one on the disclosure grid
   (`s=1.030`, cost 15.98). A better registration for that board would lower its residual,
   not raise the scratch's.

Neither limitation can rescue the statistic, because the decisive number is the scratch's
own pixel contrast at its line (58 grey, §7), far below the good maximum (168), and the
negative is unchanged under two other photometric models (§5.3).

## 5. The pre-registered statistic and the verdict

### 5.1 Definition (frozen)

- 8x8 cells, 160x90, identical to `FINE_CELL/FINE_W/FINE_H`.
- A cell is **judged** when the live mask is set, all 64 pixel positions map inside the
  reference with a valid bilinear sample, and the mapped cell centre lands on the
  reference's part (`BASE_MASK != 0`, the firmware's `ref_part_ok` rule).
- Per-cell value: `max` over the 64 pixels of `|d|`, `d` the green difference after the
  pre-registered per-board robust affine (slope `a`, offset `b`).
- **A1** = maximum over judged cells (the acceptance statistic).
- **A2** = maximum over judged cells of the cell's **4th-largest** pixel (the single-hot-
  pixel-removed variant).
- Acceptance: **0 of 40 good above the threshold** and **>= 8 of 12 scratch above it**.
  Threshold = the good maximum, so the first half is exact by construction.

### 5.2 Result

| statistic | good min / median / max | scratch min / median / max | 0-good threshold | detected | AUC [95% CI] |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A1** | 36.7 / 123.1 / **168.4** | 89.0 / 116.9 / **137.0** | 168.38 | **0/12** | **0.369** [0.200, 0.548] |
| **A2** | 32.2 / 99.1 / **136.9** | 72.3 / 94.4 / **120.6** | 136.93 | **0/12** | 0.483 [0.296, 0.671] |

The point estimate of A1 is **below chance**: a random scratch board is more likely to sit
*lower* on the statistic than a random good board. The 12 scratches are not merely
overlapping the good null; their maximum (137.0, d32) is below 12 of the 40 good boards.

ROC path (threshold strictly greater):

| threshold | good above /40 | scratch above /12 |
|---|---|---|
| > 168.4 | 0 | 0 |
| > 160 | 2 | 0 |
| > 155 | 3 | 0 |
| > 150 | 5 | 0 |
| > 145 | 7 | 0 |
| > 140 | 8 | 0 |
| > 137.0 | 12 | 1 |
| > 130 | 13 | 3 |
| > 120 | 24 | 5 |
| > 110 | 29 | 7 |
| > 100 | 34 | 9 |
| > 80 | 39 | 12 |

**Verdict: does not separate.** At the only 0-good point, 0/12; the pre-registered bar was
>= 8/12. The gap is not marginal.

![A1 on the 40 good and 12 scratch boards](pixelmax_20261029/view/A1_distribution.png)

### 5.3 Sensitivity to the photometric model (post-hoc, declared)

The pre-registered affine's slope is a regression slope between two *different* boards; it
is attenuated (0.22-0.98 here) by surface differences and sub-pixel error, and an
attenuated slope shrinks every difference, including a scratch. The same statistic was
therefore recomputed at the **same transforms** under two other photometric models. This is
a robustness check, not the verdict:

| model | good max | scratch max | detected | AUC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pre-registered robust affine | 168.4 (g40) | 137.0 (d32) | **0/12** | 0.369 |
| literal `|live - reference|` (`a=1, b=0`) | 199.7 (g40) | 195.0 (d30) | **0/12** | 0.371 |
| firmware-style mean gain + mean bias | 209.5 (g40) | 194.1 (d30) | **0/12** | 0.396 |

All three give 0/12. The literal model brings d30 to 195.0, still 4.7 grey below the good
maximum, and it raises the good maximum to 199.7 at the same time. **The negative is a
property of the population, not of the normalisation.**

## 7. Mechanism: why the maximum fails, with the numbers

### 7.1 The line's own 64-pixel maximum

d30 is the board whose scratch was located independently, by the previous study, in the
native crop: a branched pale line at cells **(45-46, 73-76)**, five deep cells reading
32/46/42/57/43 (the record's `fine_deep`). At those exact cells, on the registered pair,
under the literal `|live - reference|`:

| cell | 64-pixel mean | 64-pixel max | max/mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| (45,73) | 11.2 | 28.0 | 2.50 |
| (46,73) | 31.8 | 52.0 | 1.63 |
| (46,74) | 31.6 | 50.5 | 1.60 |
| (46,75) | 41.8 | 56.0 | 1.34 |
| (46,76) | 35.6 | 58.2 | 1.64 |

**The line's own 64-pixel maximum is 58.2 grey** (identity photometry; 27.8 under the
pre-registered affine). The good population's board-level maximum is **168.4**. The
scratch's line, measured at its own cells, is below **39 of the 40** good boards' maxima
(the only exception is g0, the board the reference was taken from, at 36.7).

The line is not a sub-tap spike: at its cells the full-cell mean of `|d|` is 32-42 and the
max is only 1.3-1.6x that. Reading the missing 28 pixels therefore buys a factor of ~1.5 on
the line -- while the board-level maximum is set 110 grey higher by something else.

### 7.2 What sets the board maximum instead

The board maximum is set by the worst *unit-to-unit* surface difference between the live
board and the one reference board: specular pads, silk edges, solder-mask gloss. On g40 the
A1 cell is (85,74) at 168.4; on d30 the A1 cell is (67,66) at 131.2 and it is **not the
line** (the line is 22 cells away). The per-pixel maximum is the wrong instrument for a
broad, low-contrast surface mark on a population whose boards differ by up to ~200 grey at
their own specular features. This is the same direction the 2026-10-29 shape study
measured at the cell level (the good boards, not the scratches, are the texture-rich
population), one level down.

![d30: the registered |live-reference| around the line, G and B](pixelmax_20261029/view/d30_line_diff.png)

The colour version of the same crop is the clearest evidence that the line is there and
that the max is not measuring it: the line is visible inside the marked box in both
channels (stronger in B), while the board's A1 cell is a different feature.

### 7.3 Why this is not a registration artefact

- The registration lowers the mean `|d|` on every board (median 57% reduction) and passes
  two exact known-answer pairs.
- The line's own maximum is 58 at its own cells under the *literal* difference; a perfect
  registration could not raise it to the good maximum without inventing 110 grey.
- The negative is unchanged under three photometric models and in all three colour
  channels (§8), which no single registration error can explain.

The registration's remaining uncertainty is therefore reported, not used as an excuse: it
cannot move the line by the 110 grey the separation would need.

## 8. The secondary question: does R or B raise the scratch's pixel contrast?

### 8.1 The pre-registered test, and why it does not answer the question

The frozen definition (§8 of the pre-registration) put the window on the **A1 cell** and
compared the line amplitude against the local background there. Result:

| channel | median contrast ratio vs G | greater than G on | criterion (median > 1.15 and >= 8/12) |
|---|---|---|---|
| R | 0.905 | 1/12 | **no** |
| B | 0.995 | 6/12 | **no** |

**The pre-registered answer is negative -- but it is not evidence about the scratch.** On
d30 the A1 cell is (67,66) and the line is at (45-46, 73-76); the window measured a
specular feature. This is a flaw in §8 of the pre-registration (the window should have been
anchored on the line, not on the statistic being tested), and it is stated here rather than
hidden. The formal negative stands for what it actually measured: *at the location the A1
statistic fires, neither R nor B raises the contrast.*

### 8.2 At the line, on the one board where it is independently located (post-hoc)

d30's five line cells, same registered pair, identity photometry, amplitude = mean of the
10 largest `|d|`, background = median `|d|` over the same 320 pixels:

| channel | amplitude | background | **contrast** | **peak** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R | 77.9 | 50.0 | **27.8** | 86.2 |
| G | 55.5 | 32.1 | **23.3** | 58.2 |
| B | 103.9 | 55.3 | **48.6** | 104.9 |

**B raises the line's contrast by 2.09x over G** (48.6 vs 23.3) and its peak by 1.80x
(104.9 vs 58.2). R raises it by only 1.19x. That is a real physical lead: the scratch
disturbs the blue/red reflectance of the green solder mask more than the green.

### 8.3 But a B-channel comparison still does not separate (post-hoc)

The same A1 statistic recomputed with R and B instead of G, at the same transforms and with
each channel's own robust affine:

| channel | good max | scratch max | detected | AUC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R | 148.6 | 137.3 | **0/12** | 0.406 |
| G | 168.4 | 137.0 | **0/12** | 0.369 |
| B | 178.2 | 173.6 (d37) | **0/12** | 0.429 |

B lifts the scratch side (d37 to 173.6, d30 to 162.3) **and** the good side (g40 to 178.2)
by about the same amount. The channel is a better *contrast* channel but not a better
*detector*: 0/12 in all three, and the AUC stays below chance.

### 8.4 The other 11 boards cannot be located from this record

A per-board line locator built from the record's `fine_deep > fine_tol` components does not
work: only d23 (5 cells, elongation 2.53), d29 (5, 1.62), d30 (5, 3.52) and d32 (6, 1.48)
have a component of 5 cells or more; the other eight boards have only 1-2 cell components
(d31 has **zero** deep cells), and the most-elongated 3-cell component is a degenerate
collinear segment, not the line. **The channel question is therefore answered only for
d30** and is *unmeasured* for the other eleven. The pre-registered 12-board number is the
formal answer and it is not informative about the line.

## 9. What this closes, and what it does not claim

**Closes:** the "maximum over the pixels of an 8x8 cell" family, on this population, at this
geometry, against this reference. The maximum was the last untested member. It fails by
0/12 at the 0-good point, in all three channels, and under three photometric models. The
previous round's cell/tap statistics failed the same way; the mechanism is now measured at
pixel level (the line is broad: 58 grey at its own cells, 1.3-1.6x the cell mean), not just
argued from the cell grid.

**Does not claim:**
- that no per-pixel statistic can ever work -- only that *this* statistic, computed this
  way on this population, does not;
- that the scratch is invisible -- it is visible, and d30's line measures 58 grey in G and
  105 in B at its own cells;
- that the channel question is settled for the other 11 boards -- it is not measured there;
- that the registration is exact -- its residuals and the `reg_scale` disagreement are
  reported in §4;
- that a production threshold exists.

## 10. What a bench confirmation would require, and what the change would cost

### 10.1 If anyone wanted to confirm this negative on a bench

The measurement is offline and reproducible from the five dumped buffers plus the 52
evidence directories. A bench confirmation would be:

1. Re-take the reference (MB_CMD = 9) on the stopped station and **dump `REF_BASE` and its
   three lattices again** with the same gates (a new reference will have a different
   fingerprint; that is the point: a new reference is a new measurement).
2. Re-run the same 12 scratch boards and >= 40 good boards, capturing the five evidence
   files per board; the same reader controls must pass 52/52.
3. Re-run `dl/pixelmax_20261029/pixelmax.py` unchanged, with the same acceptance (0/40 and
   >= 8/12). Do **not** mix the old rows with the new reference: a reference take clears
   the tolerance map and shifts the whole DAREA column.
4. Expected outcome from this measurement: 0/12 again. A bench run that produced >= 8/12
   would contradict 52 registered pairs and would need an explanation before it was
   believed.

### 10.2 If the statistic had passed, what the firmware change would be

PS-only, in the fine pass (`main.c` ~10069-10154), which already loops over every cell and
already samples the reference through `ref_at()`:

- per judged cell, read **64** live green bytes instead of 36 (the four 3x3 taps), and call
  `ref_at()` **64** times instead of 36;
- keep two new words per comparison (board maximum, cell index) and reserve two mailbox
  words from the 588..639 free block (`dl/mb_free.py`); no new DDR buffer;
- cost: **+28 live byte reads and +28 bilinear reference samples per judged cell** (+78% of
  the fine pass's sampling). At the 380-647 judged cells measured here that is 10.6k-18.1k
  extra live reads and the same number of extra `ref_at` calls per comparison, once per
  `DIFF_EVERY = 16` frames. The whole comparison measured **91.1 ms** (`MB_DIFF_MS`, Stage
  8; loop 137.4 ms), so the added cost is on the order of tens of milliseconds per
  comparison at most -- it must be measured on the build, not assumed. This is the cheap
  path: no PL re-synthesis.
- a PL implementation would need the reference's transformed sampling in the PL (the
  registration transform and bilinear sampler) plus a per-cell 64-pixel accumulator; that
  is a frame-path change, and only worth it if a *different* statistic justifies it.

**No firmware change is proposed.** The measurement that would justify it does not exist.

## 13. 中文摘要

**结论：不分离，而且方向是反的。** 预注册统计量（每个 8x8 格、全 64 像素的
`|live − reference|` 最大值，板级取所有判定格的最大）在 40 块良品上最大 **168.4**
（g40），12 块划伤板最大 **137.0**（d32）；在"零良品超过"的阈值上检出 **0/12**，
AUC **0.369**（低于 0.5，95% CI 0.200–0.548）。稳健版 A2（格内第 4 大像素）同样
**0/12**。换光度模型也不变：字面 `|live − ref|` 0/12（AUC 0.371），固件式均值增益+偏置
0/12（AUC 0.396）。现有格均值 DAREA 在同一点是 2/12，全格均值复算是 1/12。

**参考帧的身份链条（本次唯一一次 dump）：** `REF_BASE = 0x12400000`，2,764,800 字节；
固件自己的 `ref_fingerprint()`（每 1024 字节取 1 字节的 FNV-1a）读出 **0x6792A805**，
与 `TOL_HDR[4]` 和全部 79 个 `meta.json` 的 `ref_fp` 一致；dump 前后两次指纹相同；再由
dump 的参考帧重算 640x360 灰格与梯度格，与 DDR 里的 `GREY_BASE`、`BASE_GM` **逐字节
一致**（0 个不一致），`BASE_MASK` 685 格 = 全部 meta 的 `part_ref`。FWID =
`TSC1327CD597`。

**配准：** 两组合成已知答案的变换都恢复到 <0.001 px / <0.001°；52 块板的拟合残差
2.40–17.10 灰（中位 7.98），相对恒等变换降低 29%–71%；位移 0.15–56.24 px，角度
0.01–3.54°，尺度 0.9298–1.0259；没有一块被配准门槛排除。局限：拟合尺度与固件发布的
`reg_scale` 不完全一致（rms 0.028），g20 的尺度代价面多峰——已在正文写明，且不足以
改变 110 灰的差距。

**为什么失败（实测机制）：** d30 是唯一已知划伤位置的板（前一轮目视定位在格
(45-46, 73-76)）。在它自己的 5 个划伤格上，64 像素最大只有 **58.2 灰**（字面光度），
而全格均值是 32–42 灰，最大/均值只有 1.3–1.6 倍——**划痕是一条宽的、低对比度的线，
不是抽头之间的尖峰**；板级最大值由两块不同板之间的镜面/丝印差异决定（g40 168.4，
d30 的 A1 格在 (67,66)，不是划痕）。所以"读全部 64 像素"在划痕处只多买到 ~1.5 倍，
而良品侧高 70 灰以上。

**颜色通道：** 预注册的窗口（A1 格）结论为负（R 0.905、1/12；B 0.995、6/12），但该窗口
在 d30 落在镜面格而不是划痕，**它没有回答划痕的问题**——这一点在正文明确写出。在 d30
的划痕格上事后测量：G 对比度 23.3、R 27.8、B **48.6**（B 是 G 的 **2.09 倍**，峰值
104.9 vs 58.2）。但把整条规则换成 B 通道仍然 **0/12**（良品最大 178.2，划伤 d37
173.6），AUC 0.429。其余 11 块的划痕位置无法从记录里定位，**未测**。

**代价（若当初通过）：** 仅 PS 改动：每个判定格从 36 次活帧读 + 36 次 `ref_at` 增到 64
次（+78%），本批 380–647 个判定格即每次比较多 1.1 万–1.8 万次读；整次比较在 Stage 8
实测 91.1 ms，增量最多数十 ms，需在构建上实测。**不建议任何固件改动。**

## 15. Bottom line for the owner

- The reference was found, identified by its own fingerprint and its own lattice, and
  dumped once. It is the reference every one of the 79 evidence sets was compared against.
- The full-cell 64-pixel maximum was measured on the same 40 good and 12 scratch boards,
  with a registration validated by known answers and reported per board.
- **It does not separate: 0/12 at the 0-good point, AUC 0.369, in every photometric model
  and every colour channel.**
- The reason is measured: the scratch is a broad, low-contrast line whose own 64-pixel
  maximum is 58 grey, while the good boards' unit-to-unit surface differences reach 168.
- **Do not implement the full-cell maximum.** The class is closed for this family.
- The one live lead is the **B channel** (2.1x the line contrast on d30), but a B-channel
  comparison also gives 0/12, and the other 11 boards' lines cannot be located from this
  record. Testing it would need a new bench run with a per-board line locator; it is not
  justified by what is measured here.
